# Dungeon, challenge, and effect corrections (2026-09-09)

- Fortune Hunters routes `0x0167`: zero refreshes available board slots; 1?4 refresh a single slot. The authored costs are 100/30 Andermant. Accepted missions and daily attempts are preserved. Invalid slots, inactive events, and insufficient funds do not debit currency.
- Scaling monsters with `CharLevel > 1` normalize their already-levelled stats by the authored level curve before applying the destination level and difficulty. Level-one scaling coefficients and non-scaling encounters retain their existing behavior. For example, the level-14 skeleton and its scaling variant now have comparable damage at level 105; Painful adds the authored difficulty factor instead of multiplying the original level growth again.
- Scaling closed PvE dungeons promote ordinary actors across the population until ten champions/leaders are present. Their positions, actor IDs, templates, navigation, and quest identities are retained; rank health/damage/defense factors and combat rank are updated together. Dedicated boss rooms keep their authored encounter population.
- Dungeon entries have a private run identity. Matching group members share an existing run, including its combat and events; unrelated players cannot supply stale state. Empty runs release their event registry and pending start timers. Difficulty is normalized across access-ID and slider travel. Travel always provides the destination entry position; saved positions apply only to reconnecting into the same difficulty.
- Daily challenges track accepted objectives for kills (ordinary, ranked, sentinel, boss, specific templates), loot quantities, crafting, consumables, salvage, quest turn-ins, cooking boxes, PvP kills, completed battlegrounds, and wins. Difficulty restrictions are enforced. Rewards include items, level-dependent gold/honor, timed buffs, and completion-count bonuses. Completed tiers persist; the next tier requires acceptance. A full inventory retains capped progress and retries the reward on gameplay events or board selections without granting it twice.
- Daily progress uses actor-scoped `0x0095` (one ID, state byte, progress uint32, six date words), followed by the refreshed board. Native encoder evidence: `0x1409402f4 -> 0x140a53f2c`; completion state 3 is handled by `0x140234b40`.
- Frost and other victim effects retain cast identity across delayed projectile packets. Repeated impacts from one cast cannot repeatedly advance that victim?s stack counter. The bookkeeping is bounded and cleared with the map.
- Detailed finite effects cannot carry zero duration. The native counted-player branch (`0x140341fdc -> 0x140958548`) reconstructs its template and ignores wire duration overrides. Counted effects whose native duration is zero use the permanent flag plus an explicit server deadline/removal; finite gameplay duration is retained. Replacement, departure, and expiry retire the corresponding timer.

Validation includes authored dungeon populations at Normal/Painful/Fatal/Bloodshed, levelled vs scaling templates, group isolation, entry spawn, exact progress bytes, accepted-only tracking, next/final tiers, full bags, relogs, bonus payouts, refresh billing, delayed frost impacts, and explicit buff expiry. Client-side log disappearance and balance feel still require a live client retest after restarting the server.
